The Maker Philosophy

I am a founder and hands-on builder based in Mumbai. As the visionary behind Makerworkslab, I've dedicated my career to making complex technology accessible and exciting for everyone.

With a background in **Full-Stack Development** and **Embedded Systems**, I bridge the gap between digital logic and physical reality. Whether it's training young engineers or building enterprise-level automation, I focus on systems that actually work.

My approach is simple: **Learn by building.** This philosophy is at the core of everything I do, from my books to my laboratory sessions.
